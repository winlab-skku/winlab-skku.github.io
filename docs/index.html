Wireless Intelligence Lab

Intelligence for wireless networks from the ground to space. We explore next-generation wireless systems through aerial and space networking, online optimization and control, and agentic AI for autonomous network operation.

School of Electronic and Electrical Engineering, Sungkyunkwan University
Directed by Prof. Kisong Lee

Research areas

  • Aerial & Space NetworksWe study the design and optimization of aerial and space wireless networks incorporating UAVs, LEO satellites, and terrestrial systems. Our research includes UAV trajectory and deployment optimization, mobility-aware communications, satellite beam placement, and space-air-ground integrated networking.
  • Online Wireless OptimizationWe develop online optimization frameworks for wireless networks under time-varying traffic, channel, mobility, and environmental conditions. Our research focuses on Lyapunov optimization, model predictive control, real-time environment sensing and prediction, and adaptive resource management.
  • Agentic AI CommunicationsWe study agentic AI frameworks for autonomous wireless systems, where AI agents perceive the environment, reason about user and system objectives, and plan communication strategies. Our research focuses on adaptive decision-making, network control, and closed-loop learning from real-time feedback.
